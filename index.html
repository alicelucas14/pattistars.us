WHAT IS RUMMY ?

ONE PLACE

Rummy is a card game that is played with two or more players. It is usually played with two to four decks of cards and each player is dealt a certain number of cards.

TRANSPARENCY AND TRUST

The objective of the game is to form sets and sequences with the cards in your hand and discard the remaining cards.

USER

The sets and sequences can be formed by matching the same rank of cards (like two kings) or by matching the same suit of cards (like two spades).

FLEXIBILITY

The sets and sequences can also be formed by matching the same rank of cards with different suits (like a king of spades and a king of hearts).
